Brightspace Companion

One dashboard for grades, assignments, and due dates from Brightspace and Gradescope — synced automatically, right on your Mac.

⌘ Download for Mac
v0.1.0 Apple Silicon & Intel ~175 MB

Before you open it

This app isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet, so macOS will say it's from an "unidentified developer" the first time you open it. That's expected — it's not a virus warning, just Apple's default stance on any app that hasn't paid for a certificate.

Step 2 below shows you the one-time way past it.

Setup

1

Install the app

Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Brightspace Companion into your Applications folder.

2

Open it the first time

In Applications, right-click (or Control-click) Brightspace Companion and choose Open, then click Open again in the dialog that appears. You only need to do this once — after that it opens normally.

3

Add the Chrome extension

The app needs its browser extension to actually pull your grades from Brightspace and Gradescope. Install it from the Chrome Web Store.

4

Log in and sync

Open the app, add your courses in Settings, then make sure you're logged into Brightspace and Gradescope in Chrome. The extension syncs automatically in the background from there.
